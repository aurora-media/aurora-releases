Privacy

Updated: 17 September 2026

This website has no checkout, registration form, mailing list, advertising or analytics scripts. Images and fonts are served with the website.

Visiting this website

Cloudflare hosts the site and processes request information such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser information and timestamps to deliver and protect it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its processing practices. We have not configured application-level visitor tracking or a visitor database.

The site does not set application cookies or store information in your browser's local storage. Hosting and security services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Links to GitHub

Documentation and general support link to GitHub. GitHub processes visits and account activity under its privacy policy. Issues and comments in the public repository are visible to others: do not post account details, private paths, credentials or licence keys.

The Aurora application

Your library, accounts and viewing progress are stored on your own Aurora server. Services used for metadata, artwork, subtitles, updates or external sources receive the requests needed for those features. Their policies apply to those requests. Licence signatures are verified locally.

Payments

This site does not collect payment or order information: nothing is sold. Voluntary donations go through GitHub Sponsors, under GitHub's privacy policy; this site never sees them.

General questions

You can ask non-sensitive questions in the public project tracker. It is not a channel for sharing personal information.
